🔥 WHEN TO USE ROCIEVN
✅ Use ROCIEVN when:
● You need expert-level answers
● The problem is complex or personal
● You don't want to iterate 10 times
● You want answers tailored to YOUR situation
● Quality matters more than speed

✅ Skip elements when:

● You need a quick, simple answer
● You're exploring (not decided yet)
● You don't have context yet

📌 KEY INSIGHTS  
Short prompt = Generic answer
Long, structured prompt = Expert answer
